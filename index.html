Current Projects

Automating Scanning Electrochemical Microscopy: Decision Making in The Loop

This project proposes the development of an autonomous guidance system for Scanning Electrochemical Microscopy (SECM) to improve efficiency in the investigation of structures of interest. Conventional SECM experiments require full-area scanning and post-acquisition data processing, which is time-consuming when the structures are sparsely distributed. The central objective is to integrate real-time electrochemical simulations and control algorithms to enable adaptive probe navigation.

Volumetric Titrations with (Non-)Visual Indicators: How Low-Cost Assistive Technology Can Make Existing Experiments More Accessible

This project proposes a framework that allows experiments that depend on a specific sensory modality, such as volumetric titrations, to be translated into other sensory stimuli, such as sound, and used to support accessible education in accordance with the principles of Universal Design for Learning.

In July 2026, I visited the Krennert Art Museum where a giant crochet coral reef installation was on display, created by Christine Wertheim and Margaret Wertheim. It was so colorful and creative that I instantly felt the need to know how it was made. So I went to the The Idea Store, bought my first hook and yarn, and googled "How to Crochet", the first few days were terrible, but soon I was able to follow a tutorial to crochet my own hyperbolic coral!
